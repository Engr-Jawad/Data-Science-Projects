# Key Insights and Findings

## 1. Regional Analysis
- Swat was the most frequently occurring region in the dataset.
- Charsadda recorded the highest temperature, reaching 44°C.
- Mardan had the highest average rainfall, at approximately 61 mm.
- Dir recorded the highest irrigation value, at 420 mm.
- Peshawar had the highest total water consumption in the dataset, with 65,729,700 liters.
- Dir had the lowest total water consumption, with 394,880 liters.

## 2. Temperature and Rainfall Analysis
- The average temperature across the dataset was approximately 24°C.
- The average rainfall was approximately 60 mm.
- The maximum recorded rainfall was 260 mm.
- The temperature data showed no outliers using the selected outlier-detection method.
- The temperature distribution appeared approximately normal, based on the initial analysis. Further testing would be needed to confirm normality.

## 3. Water Usage and Farm Area
- The average water consumption was approximately 17.73 million liters per record, according to the calculated mean.
- The average farm area was approximately 6.24 hectares.
- Peshawar had the highest reported water consumption, while Dir had the lowest.
- The water-consumption distribution was right-skewed, meaning that a smaller number of observations had substantially higher water usage than most observations.

## 4. Outlier Analysis
The analysis identified the following numbers of potential outliers using the selected method:

- Irrigation: 143 outliers.
- Water consumption: 181 outliers.
- Farm area: 181 outliers.
- Crop yield: 532 outliers.
- Soil moisture: 568 outliers.

These values identify observations that differ considerably from the rest of the data. However, an outlier is not necessarily an error; it may represent an unusual but valid observation.

## 5. Crop Yield Analysis
- Sugarcane had the highest average crop yield per hectare among the crops in the dataset.
- Cotton had the lowest average crop yield per hectare.
- June showed the highest average yield among the calendar months.
- March showed the lowest average yield among the calendar months.

These comparisons are based on the calculated averages. Other factors, including crop type, weather, irrigation, and farm conditions, would need to be considered before making agricultural recommendations.

## 6. Statistical Findings
- The correlation between rainfall and total crop yield was approximately 0.059, indicating a very weak positive linear relationship in this dataset.
- The water-consumption data showed a right-skewed distribution.
- The temperature data appeared approximately normally distributed, although additional statistical testing would be needed to confirm this.
- Outlier detection helped identify unusual observations in several numerical columns.

## 7. Overall Conclusion
The analysis explored regional weather conditions, rainfall, irrigation, water consumption, farm area, soil moisture, and crop yield. Sugarcane had the highest average yield per hectare, while cotton had the lowest. Water consumption varied considerably across observations, and the relationship between rainfall and total crop yield was very weak.

These findings provide a useful summary of the dataset and demonstrate how statistical analysis and visualization can help identify patterns. However, because the dataset is synthetic, the results should be treated as practice findings rather than evidence of actual agricultural conditions. Further analysis using real agricultural data would be necessary to develop reliable recommendations for farmers.
